For further information on any items, the help command available in-game can be used to get more in-depth details.

Equipment

Not all pieces of equipment can be used by all miners--Jezzers cannot use items worn on the legs, and Bolos are much too large to use any worn items designed for smaller races. Most types of equipment have designated slots, and only one item per slot can be worn--you cannot wear multiple packs, or multiple belts, etc.

  • A variety of digging implements can be used to help clear dirt faster, from the simple digger spade to the more advanced sonic shovel and exclusive, high-tech disruptor pick.
  • Backpacks can be worn to help a miner carry extra weight. Some teams have access to the higher-tech and more effective hover pack, which can carry even more weight.
  • Belt packs also help carry a little extra weight when worn.
  • Power packs and power belts boost the maximum amount of energy that can be stored at once.
  • Power bracers, hyper belts, and speed boosters are all pieces of equipment designed to boost a miner's raw physical performance.
  • Besseltech Enterprises offers experimental equipment such as the cerebro helmet to boost mental acuity and nanofiber gloves that increase manual skill. Both of these attributes are important for using sensitive technological equipment to its best effect.
  • Energy cyclers are handy items that can be equipped with any other items, and generate power from environmental sources such as waste heat and background radioactivity. They can even generate power from radioactive kaburite ore, although carrying large amounts of kaburite around for this purpose is not generally recommended.
  • Scuba gear can be worn to allow miners to swim through water (slowly) and dive to recover underwater ore. Mobbels do not need scuba gear.
Weapons

All of these firearms are used by equipping them and clicking on a spot on the map to fire. Some are more accurate than others, but all are affected by the attacker's aim scores. Some weapons require ammo to be loaded; others are energy weapons that drain power from the user's energy meter.

  • Pistols tend to be mediocre weapons but are inexpensive and widely available, and are the only weapons that can be used while swimming underwater. The Azzik 58R is weapon of choice for cheap thugs, while the LI-89 gelpistol uses nonlethal force to avoid messy (and illegal!) deaths. The Jevco EP-950 magpistol and Z8 laser pistol are a bit more expensive and less common alternatives that pack a bigger punch against armored opponents.
  • Larger weapons are bulkier, more expensive, and less common, but in general quite a bit more powerful than light handguns. The Jevco SA-7 assault rifle is a well-rounded semi-automatic weapon that's not too hard to get and has many tactical applications. SO-83 slag rifles are light anti-armor weapons, slow to fire but packing quite a punch; unfortunately, they're only available to a few teams. The U6-AP sonic disruptor is a high tech, nonlethal energy weapon available to many of the higher-tech teams; while it's not the most powerful weapon, it has the handy ability to fire through solid matter. BEM5 rad rifles are terrifying, highly illegal weapons that can covertly kill opponents with radiation; only The Jagrinki are so despicable as to use them. The W7 plasma rifle is one of the most powerful personal weapons in existance, but is exclusively available to the military Imperial Kadazil Mining Federation and drains energy very, very rapidly.
  • For those few teams that have access to such things, heavy weapons may be bulky and difficult for weaker races to carry but can provide some serious firepower in a hurry. The LI-170P riot cannon is a powerful crowd-control weapon designed to provide withering but nonlethal firepower; it's difficult to match at close ranges, but chews through ammo in a hurry. The G-553 rocket launcher is a straightforward but devastating weapon; it's heavy, slow to load, and difficult to fire at individual targets, but makes a pretty big hole in anything it hits.
Ammo
  • 58 standard rounds are used by the Azzik 58R alone. They're relatively cheap and come in clips of 12.
  • Gel cartridges are 30-round clips used by the gelpistol and riot cannon.
  • Mag clips contain ferrous bullets fired by the magpistol and assault rifle. They have 30 rounds each and are moderately expensive.
  • Slag rounds are expensive specialized ammo for the slag rifle's 20-round clip.
  • Rockets are heavy individual rockets for use with the rocket launcher.
Armor

As with other equipment, armor is restricted to one item per equipment "slot", and not all slots are useable by all races. Note that the radiation suit counts as its own slot and can be worn over anything (but not by bolos, who are still much too large for any armor save their own tough hides).

  • A wide variety of body armor is available to help make sure miners don't have any "accidents" down in the tunnels. Cheap, light impact vests are available to the civilian population and provide some defense against light arms. Blast jackets are mediocre protection against most attacks but can absorb explosions much more effectively than other armor. Flex jackets are effective, moderately light pieces of standard infantry gear, but are unfortunately unavailable to most teams. Durosteel plate is nearly indestructable, but heavy and clumsy armor. Nanomesh armor is cutting-edge experimental gear developed by Besseltech Enterprises that combines effective physical protection with a built-in light forcefield generator and reactive materials that give unparalleled freedom of movement. The combat suit is a full body armor used by the Imperial Kadazil Mining Federation that administers performance-enhancing stimulants at the same time it provides the best physical defense available.
  • Shield belts and force packs are personal forcefield generators that are effective even against weapons designed to defeat conventional physical armor, but which drain energy to operate.
  • Miner's caps are primitive but effective protection on the cheap that have been used by miners since ancient times. Leg guards are similarly simple, light, and cheap defense, but are ignored by most teams. The advanced attack helmet is designed to boost accuracy with firearms and general combat performance, as well as provide vital defense.
  • Radiation suits are heavy pieces of protection designed to fit easily over clothes or even body armor. Rad suits aren't necessarily designed to protect the wearer from weapons and are slow and clumsy when worn, but absorb nearly all background radiation and are the only completely effective means of protecting oneself from the lethal effects of the rad rifle.
Miscellaneous items

These items include expendable one-use items as well as gadgets that drain energy. All of them are used by selecting them in your inventory and clicking on a target. It's important to have both a high dexterity as well as a high intelligence to use these items most effectively.

  • Med patches and the more effective biofix kits are easy-to-administer medical gear for use in the field. In addition to restoring nearly all health, biofix kits can provide some slight relief from radiation, and temporarily boosts the recipient's natural rate of healing.
  • Several varieties of explosives are available for blasting open large areas of dirt (or large areas of rival miners!) Heavy blast packs can be set with a short fuse, then detonate with a wide, powerful explosive. Smaller micro-charges are detonated by a short-range signal; once set, click on them for a medium-strength, tightly focused blast. Grenades also have a similar, moderately small explosion, but can be thrown a good distance instead of planted; unfortunately, such weapons are not available to most teams.
  • Battery charges can be used to quickly recharge a portion of your energy meter.
  • In addition to radiation suits, there exist several other methods of fighting deadly radiation. Radiation pills can be taken to provide several minutes of resistance to background radiation, and portable decontamination units can clean radiation from a miner or their teammates, although it drains power to do so.
  • Inexpensive locator devices can be used by a clever miner to quickly pinpoint the locations of their teammates with a slight energy drain, as well as home in on the location of their home base. The more expensive, higher tech comm scanner can do so as well, but is also capable of intercepting the location of enemy miners, albeit at lower ranges and a higher energy cost.
  • The omni-tool is a curious looking but very useful gizmo. Omni-tools are in effect an entire toolkit compacted into a simple handheld gadget, and can be used for a variety of purposes: it can repair your team's vehicles and installations (or damage those of your enemies), as well as disarm hidden mines or alarms. Be aware, though, that as with all such gadgets, using an omni-tool effectively requires high intelligence and dexterity scores--clumsy miners are likely to do more harm than good.
  • Personal teleporters are wonderful handheld devices that can be tuned to teleport to any telepad controlled by the user's team, but they drain an enormous amount of energy!
  • Orefinders are advanced gadgets that scan the screen for ore buried deep inside dirt at a small energy cost. Orefinders are not 100% accurate, especially when used by an inept miner.
Installations

Installations are heavy, semipermanent constructions that can be set up to perform various functions. Some are more portable than others and can be disassembled to take to a new location once installed.

  • Energy fences consist of several generator poles which can be set up in a line to create a powerful barrier attuned to a team's communicator frequencies, letting teammates through safely but shocking any enemies that attempt to pass.
  • Homing beacons are small, cheap devices that can be quickly set up to broadcast a signal for any teammates with a locator device.
  • Monocart rails are rigid cable lengths used by monocarts as tracks.
  • Bridges are light but remarkably sturdy and durable floating platforms that can be assembled for easy transport over water.
  • Med stations are portable field hospitals capable of synthesizing their own supplies, which can be set up very quickly and used by teammates to heal when away from base.
  • Generators are portable power sources that teammates can use to recharge their energy meter quickly.
  • Radiation scrubbers are devices used by the Pan-Galactic Eco Mining Cooperative to absorb harmful radiation. Rad scrubbers not only reduce the general radioactivity of an area, but can quickly decontaminate miners close by.
  • Although expensive and very power-draining, teleportation devices can be used for instantaneous transport between locations. A fully operational teleport station consists of a teleport console and telepad installed immediately adjacent each other; the console can be used to teleport the contents of its adjacent telepad to any other telepad controlled by the same team, but drains large amounts of energy to do so. Teleconsoles can be recharged from miners' personal energy supply when they run out of power.
  • Microrefineries are special devices employed only by Dailim Mining, Inc. that can be used to process a quantity of low-grade ores into a smaller, lighter quantity of high-grade ore. Use the insert command to fill the microrefinery with poor or medium ore, then activate the microrefinery to strip it down to rich ore (if possible). Note that the microrefinery doesn't actually increase the total value of ore! It compresses poor and medium ore to a lighter weight but equally valuable amount of rich ore.
Vehicles

Vehicles are the fastest way of getting around in Lode Wars. Use the board and exit commands to get in and out of vehicles; when driving, steer with the arrow keys as you would walk. Pressing the same direction multiple times causes a vehicle to go faster; pressing backwards will slow down. Numpad 5 will slow down and brake to a stop without turning a vehicle around. Some vehicles can carry ore in addition to passengers; use the deposit command to dump your ore into the bin of such a vehicle to drive it back to base faster.

  • Tunnel buggies are small, fast one-man vehicles built for driving over rugged ground and in cramped spaces. Tunnel buggies are quick and maneuverable, but not very durable.
  • Flathaulers are heavy-duty transport vehicles with a rear bed capable of carrying large amounts of ore or even an extra passenger.
  • Monocarts are fast, cheap, and durable vehicles capable of carrying large amounts of ore very quickly, but can only travel over pre-built rails.
  • Power rafts are small, portable water craft that are light and cheap but relatively vulnerable.
